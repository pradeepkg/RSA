Okta✓ Verified

March 15, 2024 · 10 min read
Pradeep Kadambar
Creator

Okta is a cloud-based IAM platform that offers secure user authentication, SSO, and authorization. It simplifies identity management, access control, and provisioning for organizations. With Okta, businesses can enhance security, streamline user experiences, and enable seamless access to applications and data. It supports multifactor authentication and integrations with various systems, enabling secure digital transformation and centralized identity management.

Versions: 8.0.0 P1

Overview​

It is recommended that you review the Okta Collector Guide before proceeding with this connector configuration.

Connector Settings​

Steps

  1. Login to RSA Identity G&L console as System Administrator.
  2. Navigate to AFX > Connectors.
  3. Click on Create Connector and select the RESTful Web Service connector template.
  4. Enter the details as shown below under General tab.

  5. Click on the Settings tab and enter the required connection details.

  6. Click on Add More Parameters.
  7. Enter the details for scope as shown below and click OK.

  8. Provide the offline_access okta.users.manage okta.groups.manage in the Scope field.
  9. Similarly add another parameter state with value rsagl.
  10. Click on Get OAuth 2.0 Access Token and authorize.
  11. Click OK.
